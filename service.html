Fundraise for Food

The empty shelves in our pantry represent not just a lack of food, but a void in the lives of those who rely on it. Your support can help us replenish these stores and ensure that no one goes hungry.

Clean Water

Clean Drinking Water

73% of the people in our community face this daily. There are no nearby boreholes — people make long lines just to fetch 1 or 2 jerry cans from a distant pond, leading to poor sanitation and hygiene.

Young children aged 3–15 collect water from dirty ponds that easily spread diseases like cholera, dysentery, and athlete's foot. Children walk close to 3 km just to reach these water sources. Many people have resorted to dirty water simply to avoid the long journey.

What hurts most is that people with bicycles and motorcycles sell water at high prices — a 20-litre jerry can costs $1–2. Let's join hands and fundraise to construct more boreholes and bring water closer to the community.

Maternal Health

Save Pregnant Mothers

Supporting pregnant youth is crucial for ensuring their well-being and that of their unborn children. These young mothers face unique challenges as they navigate pregnancy and early parenthood.

By offering comprehensive support services, including medical care, education, and emotional guidance, we can empower them to make informed choices and provide a nurturing environment for their children.
