We Need Your Help

Your time, skills, and passion can transform the lives of Uganda's most vulnerable children. Join our team today.

Why Volunteer

Join Our Wonderful Team of Volunteers

Would you like to join our wonderful volunteers? ECAOUg could gratefully use your talents and time. What help do we need? Some suggestions are listed below, but often volunteers bring ideas we hadn't even thought about — this list is ready for you to add your own!

We welcome people from all walks of life, whether you can give a few hours or make a long-term commitment. Every contribution counts.

Ways You Can Help

Fundraising Social Media Networking Teaching Building Wells Contacting Businesses Church Outreach Record Keeping School Engagement Storytelling

Volunteer Benefits

Personal Growth

Develop empathy, compassion, and a sense of purpose as you contribute to improving the lives of orphaned children in Uganda.

Cultural Immersion

Immerse yourself in Ugandan culture, gaining a deeper understanding of local customs, traditions, and community challenges.

Community

Share knowledge and best practice with peers from around the world who share your passion for change.

Personal Fulfilment

Making a positive impact on the lives of orphaned children brings deep fulfilment and reinforces the importance of giving back.
