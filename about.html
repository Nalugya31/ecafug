Our Story

Welcome to Exodus Community Awakening Organization Uganda

Exodus Community Awakening Organization Uganda (ECAOUg), UG Reg No. 80034820673224, is located 2.7km off Bujjuko Mityana Road in Magoma Village. As a faith-based Christian organisation, ECAOUg seeks to provide Education, Love, Hope, and Care for the orphaned and abandoned children of Uganda, reflecting the heart of Jesus Christ who said, "Let the little children come to me" (Matthew 19:14).

AIDS has wiped out an entire generation of parents, forcing thousands of children to never receive an education, parental love, or care. No education. No HOPE. But through God's mercy, we are answering the call to be hands and feet of Christ. We currently support 25 children aged between 3 to 15 years old at our Home, with an additional 45 children in our outreach program. By God's provision, we make sure all their basic needs are met, and each child is enrolled in either a local village school, day school, or boarding school.

Orphans and abandoned children are always confronted with immense psychological and social problems as they grow older. They remain vulnerable to maltreatment, economic exploitation, and sexual exploitation due to lack of care and protection. The HIV and AIDS scourge, compounded by high poverty levels, has aggravated the situation of orphans in Uganda.

With an economically weakened traditional African extended family system that can no longer effectively address this burden, most children find themselves without proper social support following the death of their parents. This denies them access to healthcare, education, shelter, and nutrition. Yet God has not forgotten them. As written in James 1:27, "Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress."

Through prayer, Christ-centred care, and the generosity of His people, we are fighting for every child — offering not just survival, but hope, dignity, and a future in God's kingdom.
