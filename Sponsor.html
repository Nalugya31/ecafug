Sponsor a Child Program — ECAOUg

An Opportunity to Invest in Potential. A Chance to Change a Life.

Sponsoring a child through Exodus Community Awakening Organization (ECAOUg) is more than helping a child survive — it's investing in a future leader, a world-changer, and a child full of God-given potential.

What ECAOUg Is Doing To Help

We carefully and prayerfully select each child in this program. These are children who have lost hope of continuing school due to:

  • Loss of parents
  • Extreme vulnerability
  • Lack of school fees and essential supplies
  • Risk of dropping out of school

Many of these children face difficult circumstances including loss of parents to AIDS, abusive environments, exploitation, neglect, and poverty.

That's where you come in.

What Sponsored Children Receive

All sponsored children live at the ECAOUg children's home or are part of our outreach program, where they receive:

  • Three nutritious meals every day
  • A safe, healthy, Christ-centered environment
  • A proper bed and bedding
  • Academic support and tutoring
  • Leadership and spiritual growth opportunities

Sponsorship Details

Your sponsorship provides everything a child needs to succeed:

$40 / month $370 / year

Annual payments are discounted to reduce processing fees associated with monthly giving.

Your Sponsorship Provides

  • Three meals a day
  • Safe housing and care in a Christ-centered home
  • School books and supplies
  • Quality education
  • A nurturing and loving environment

The Impact of Your Support

When you sponsor a child through ECAOUg's program, you're not just meeting needs — you're unlocking potential. You're giving a child the opportunity to become everything God created them to be.
